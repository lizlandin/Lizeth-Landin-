ABOUT THE ARTIST

HER CHILDHOOD

Billie Eilish Pirate Baird O'Connell was born on December 18, 2001, in Los Angeles, California. She grew up in a creative family and was homeschooled alongside her older brother, FINNEAS.

Music has been part of her life since she was little. She joined the Los Angeles Children's Chorus when she was eight and started writing songs at a young age.

HER PERSONAL STYLE

Outside of music, Billie is known for her unique fashion sense. She loves experimenting with different looks, from oversized clothes to bold hair colors.

Her style has become an important part of how she expresses herself, and she isn't afraid to try something different.

FAMILY & MUSIC

Billie is very close to her family, especially her brother FINNEAS, who is also her main musical collaborator.

They started making music together when they were young. FINNEAS produced "Ocean Eyes", the song that helped Billie become famous in 2015.

They continue writing and producing music together, and their creative partnership has been an important part of Billie's career.

CAREER HIGHLIGHTS

Since releasing "Ocean Eyes", Billie has become one of the most recognized artists of her generation.

  • 2015 — Released "Ocean Eyes".
  • 2019 — Released her debut studio album.
  • 2020 — Won Album of the Year at the Grammys.
  • 2022 — Won an Oscar for "No Time to Die".
  • 2024 — Won an Oscar for "What Was I Made For?".

MY FAVORITES

Three Billie Eilish songs I especially enjoy.

01

watch

What I love most about watch is its lyrics. They're really deep, and I remember discovering the song through TikTok edits, usually about couples or heartbreaking scenes from movies and TV shows. There's also an instrumental part that I absolutely love. For a long time, I had this song on repeat because it's exactly the kind of music that helps me concentrate. It's calm enough that it doesn't distract me, but not so slow that it makes me sleepy or bored.

02

lovely (with Khalid)

I discovered lovely when I was in middle school. We had keyboard classes, and our teacher taught us how to play it, so I spent so many afternoons listening to it over and over until I could play it properly. I honestly don't even remember if I ever learned the whole song, but the fact that I never got tired of hearing it says a lot. I also love the music video, and the way Billie's and Khalid's voices sound together is incredible. Even after all these years, I still listen to it a lot.

03

ocean eyes

I'm pretty sure ocean eyes was the first Billie Eilish song I ever heard, and probably the first one I saved too. What amazes me is how young she was when she recorded it. Her voice has always sounded so unique to me, but in this song it almost sounds angelic. I feel like anyone who listens to it can understand what I mean. The lyrics are beautiful too, and this song will always be special to me because it's what made me start listening to Billie in the first place.
